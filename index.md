# Pinote

The pinned note for your Lock Screen.

Write a note once. It arrives on every Apple device through iCloud. Pin it as a Live Activity, and a single next item waits for you on your Lock Screen — quietly.

## What it does

- Pin one note to your Lock Screen as a Live Activity
- Tap the Dynamic Island or Lock Screen to advance to the next item
- Recurring and scheduled todos that the app remembers — without rules
- Syncs across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch through iCloud

## What makes it different

**Pin, don't browse.** A single active note shows a single next item. This is not a limitation — it's the product.

**Zero setup.** No login, no Shortcuts, no configuration. Download, write, pin. Value in under 60 seconds.

**The Lock Screen stays yours.** Ads live in Library and Settings only — never on your Lock Screen, Live Activity, Dynamic Island, widgets, or watchOS Smart Stack.
