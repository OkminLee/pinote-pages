# Pinote — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-04-25

## What Pinote collects from you

Pinote does not require an account or login. Your notes are stored:
- **On your device** in Core Data (Apple's local database)
- **In your iCloud Private Database** managed by Apple's CloudKit

We (the developer) do not have access to the content of your notes. Apple's iCloud terms apply to your synced data.

## Anonymous data we do collect (for app quality)

| Service | Data | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Amplitude** | Anonymous device identifier, app event names (e.g., `note_pinned`, `note_completed`), session duration | Understand which features are useful |
| **Firebase Crashlytics** (by Google) | Crash reports, performance metrics, OS/device model | Diagnose and fix bugs |
| **Google AdMob** | Advertising identifier (only if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission), ad impressions, click data | Show ads in Library and Settings only — never on your Lock Screen |

## Third parties named explicitly

- **Amplitude** — [https://amplitude.com/privacy](https://amplitude.com/privacy)
- **Firebase Crashlytics, by Google** — [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google AdMob** — [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Apple CloudKit** — Apple's terms apply to your iCloud data

## App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

Pinote requests App Tracking Transparency permission only when you first interact with an ad surface (Library or Settings). If you decline, AdMob will show non-personalized ads only. You can change this at any time in iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Pinote**.

## Your rights

- **Delete all your data** — Open Pinote → Settings → "Delete All My Data". This wipes notes from your device and your iCloud Private Database.
- **Disable analytics** — Open iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements** to opt out at the OS level.
- **Disable ad tracking** — see ATT section above.
- **Contact us** — [Support page](/pinote-pages/support)

## Data retention

| Type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Notes | Until you delete them (locally or via "Delete All My Data") |
| Anonymous analytics events | 90 days, then aggregated and anonymized |
| Crash reports | 90 days |
| Ad data | Per Google AdMob's policy (linked above) |

## Children's privacy

Pinote is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
