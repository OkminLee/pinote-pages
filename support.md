# Pinote — Support

Need help with Pinote?

- **Response time:** 48 hours typical

## Common questions

**Why is my note not syncing to my iPad / Apple Watch?**
- Confirm both devices are signed into the same iCloud account
- Confirm iCloud Drive is on for Pinote (iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud)
- Wait up to 30 seconds for the Live Activity push to arrive

**How do I delete all my data?**
Open Pinote → Settings → "Delete All My Data". This wipes notes from your device and your iCloud Private Database.

**How do I disable ads?**
Pinote shows ads in Library and Settings only — never on your Lock Screen, Live Activity, Dynamic Island, widgets, or watchOS. A Pro tier (with ads removed) ships in a future update; Founding Users get it free for life.

**Why does the Live Activity sometimes disappear after a long idle time?**
iOS Live Activities have system-imposed time limits (typically 8 hours of inactivity). Pinote re-pins automatically when you next open the app. If your Live Activity is stuck, force-quit and reopen Pinote.

**Can I import notes from another app?**
Not in v1. Pinote is intentionally minimal — it stores plain text + checklists. You can paste manually.
